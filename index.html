1 Overview

CETverse is built and operated by Rushikesh Ramesh Gholve, trading as CETverse, based in Maharashtra, India ("CETverse", "we", "us"). We provide previous-year question papers, chapter-wise mock tests, performance tracking, and a student leaderboard for MHT-CET aspirants.

This policy covers the personal data the app and its backend services collect when you create an account, take a test, or make a purchase. It does not cover third-party sites you reach by following a link out of the app.

2 Information we collect

We collect only what the app needs to run your account, show your progress, and process a purchase.

CategoryWhat's includedWhen it's collected
Account & profile Mobile number, name, school, city, date of birth, education level (11th/12th) When you sign up or edit your profile

Support messages Whatever you write to us, plus your email address When you email cetverse@gmail.com

We do not access your contacts, camera, microphone, or location, and the app does not show third-party ads.

3 How we use it

  • Run your account: sign you in, show your tests, and save your answers and scores.
  • Show your standing: compute your rank score, percentile against past-year papers, and your position on the leaderboard.
  • Process payments and keep a record of what plan you're on and when it was purchased.
  • Apply referral coupons and credit the right referral partner.
  • Respond when you contact us for support.
  • Diagnose crashes and fix bugs reported through Google Play.

We do not use your data to build an advertising profile, and we do not sell it.

4 Who we share it with

We share data only with the services that keep CETverse running, each acting on our behalf:

Google FirebaseStores your account, test history, and leaderboard entries, and hosts the app's backend functions.
RazorpayProcesses subscription payments. We receive confirmation of payment, not your card or bank details.
2Factor.inDelivers the one-time password (OTP) SMS used to verify your mobile number at login.
Google Play servicesSupplies the on-device phone-number hint used to pre-fill the login screen.

We may also disclose data if required by law, a court order, or a government request, or to protect the rights and safety of CETverse and its users.

5 How long we keep it

We keep your account and test history for as long as your account is active, so your progress and leaderboard history stay intact across sessions. If you ask us to delete your account (see Section 6), we remove your profile, test history, and leaderboard entry; a referral partner's own payout records are kept, with your identifying details removed, since those belong to the partner's account rather than yours.

6 Your rights & deleting your account

You can ask us, at any time, to:

  • See a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate profile details.
  • Delete your account and the personal data tied to it.

To do any of these, email cetverse@gmail.com from the address or number linked to your account. We verify who you are before acting on the request, and we confirm by email once it's done. We aim to complete deletion requests within 30 days.

7 Children's privacy

CETverse is built for students preparing for MHT-CET, typically in 11th or 12th standard. The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from a child under 13 without a parent or guardian's consent. If you believe a child has given us data without that consent, contact us and we'll delete it.

8 Security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access to the underlying database is restricted to systems that operate the app. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your data against unauthorised access, alteration, or loss.

9 Changes to this policy

If we make a material change to how we collect or use your data, we'll update the date at the top of this page and, where the change is significant, show a notice inside the app. Continuing to use CETverse after a change takes effect means you accept the updated policy.
